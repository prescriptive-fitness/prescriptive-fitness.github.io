The owners

Paul and Sandy Sklar

Founder · Owner

Paul Sklar

CSCS (NSCA) · Certified Exercise Physiologist (ACSM) · TRX

Paul founded Prescriptive Fitness and has been a professional trainer since 1993. He holds a BS in Exercise and Sports Science from Wake Forest University, where he was a scholarship athlete and All-ACC selection in the steeplechase, and went on to become the #2-ranked professional duathlete in the USA and a three-time Team USA World Championship team member.

He is a recognised authority on weight loss, cardiovascular training and strength conditioning. Men's Health named him one of the Top 50 Fitness Professionals in America. He has written for Men's Fitness, served as the fitness expert for Charlotte Parent's Fit Family Challenge, and appeared on NBC's Charlotte Today.

His clients have ranged from executives and parents to former Wake Forest head basketball coach Dino Gaudio, NFL first-round picks Sean Gilbert and Tyler Brayton, NCAA Final Four referee Jamie Luckie, and two-time NCAA and NBA champion Nazr Mohammed. Paul takes on a select number of clients who are committed to making a change.

Co-owner

Sandy Sklar

NASM Certified Personal Trainer · Corrective Exercise Specialist · Pre/Post-Natal · TRX

Sandy is the co-owner of Prescriptive Fitness and a highly regarded strength, conditioning and weight-loss specialist, with more than two decades in the industry as a trainer, studio owner and coach.

After graduating from North Carolina State University she competed in World Tri-Fitness, placing first in Grace and Physique three times. She specializes in both individual and group training.

The coaches

Experience you can feel.

Former college athletes, exercise physiologists, a NASCAR driver's trainer and Titleist-certified golf specialists. Tap a coach to read more.

Marc Arnone

CSCS

Functional training · speed & agility · post-rehab

Read bio

Marc has coached at Prescriptive since 2002. He holds a BS in Exercise Sport Science from Elon, where he played defensive back from 1996 to 1999, and spent three years at HealthSouth physical therapy building weight-training programs for rehabilitation patients.

He has trained children, adults, active older adults, an Olympic athlete and professionals from the NFL, NBA and NASCAR, and in 2006 was hired as full-time personal trainer to NASCAR driver Tony Stewart before returning to Prescriptive in 2007.

Joe Schriffen

TPI Certified Golf Fitness

Golf fitness · leads the golf program

Read bio

Joe leads Prescriptive's golf program. A Springfield College graduate in Exercise Science, he has been a personal trainer since 1996 and certified through the Titleist Performance Institute since 2007.

He builds an individual plan for every golfer, whatever their level, and has worked with aspiring professionals, former LPGA players and collegiate golfers. He also brings nutritional counseling and massage therapy to his clients' programs.

Joe Schuster

NASM · TPI Fitness Coach

Golf performance · functional training · fat loss

Read bio

Joe is certified through NASM and as a Titleist Performance Institute Fitness Coach. He focuses on correcting the physical imbalances that cause inconsistency on the course, and on adding power and speed to the swing.

A multisport athlete growing up, he specializes in functional training, sport-specific training and fat loss, with an emphasis on training safely so clients get stronger, more mobile and less injury-prone.

Jane Hanisch

ACSM EP · NASM
