Common questions

I'm not an athlete. Is personal training for me?

Yes. Whether you're an athlete or just want to look, feel and move better, your program is designed around your own goals.

I'm coming back from an injury.

Rehab from a serious injury is one of the goals our trainers help clients reach. Several of our trainers specialize in post-rehab and corrective exercise. Let us know when you get in touch.

Can my spouse and I train together?

Yes, with semi-private training for two to four people. Sessions are purchased together with your training partners. We don't pair clients who don't know each other.

Can I go straight to work afterwards?

Yes. The studio has showers.

How do I get started?

Send us a message online or call (980) 209-0410 and we'll be in touch.
