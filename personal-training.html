Questions we hear a lot

I haven't trained in years. Is this for me?

Yes. Your program starts from your assessment, not from where anyone else is. Every exercise is coached, and the plan moves at the pace your body is ready for.

I have an old injury, a bad back or a joint replacement.

Tell us at your consultation. Several of our coaches specialise in corrective exercise and post-rehab training, and your program will be built around what your doctor or physical therapist has advised.

How often should I train?

It depends on your goals and your schedule. Your coach will recommend a frequency after your assessment, and adjust it as you progress.

Can my spouse and I train together?

Yes. That's exactly what semi-private training is for. Bring one to three partners and you'll share the session and the rate, with programming for each of you.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, athletic shoes and water. We have showers on site if you're heading to work or the course afterwards.

How do I get started?

Book a consultation online or call (980) 209-0410. We'll talk through your goals, match you with a coach, and set up your assessment.
