How it works

Every program starts with a prescription.

The name isn't a slogan. It's the method: we assess before we prescribe, and we re-assess before we change anything.

  1. Rx01

    Assess

    A conversation and a movement screen: your history, old injuries, strength, mobility, balance and what you want to be able to do.

  2. Rx02

    Prescribe

    Your coach writes a plan for your body and your schedule: what to train, how often, and how we'll measure it.

  3. Rx03

    Train

    Private or semi-private sessions of 30, 45 or 60 minutes, coached rep by rep by someone who knows your plan.

  4. Rx04

    Re-assess

    We test again, look at what changed and adjust. Progress you can see, not guesswork.
