Golf fitness
TPI certified golf fitness

Improve your golf game.

The Prescriptive Fitness Golf Training Program is led by a Titleist Performance Institute certified instructor. It uses the TPI 12-step assessment to identify any weakness or physical limitation in your swing, then prescribes the strength and conditioning plan to improve six key elements:

  • Balance
  • Power
  • Coordination
  • Stability
  • Mobility
  • Endurance
