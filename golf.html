The program

Six things decide your swing.

If your body can't make the move, no lesson will fix it for long. Our golf program is designed to improve the six physical elements that shape every golf swing.

Balance

Staying centred and in control from takeaway to finish.

Power

Club speed generated from the ground up, not the arms.

Coordination

Hips, torso, arms and club firing in the right order.

Stability

A strong, quiet base that lets everything else rotate.

Mobility

The hip and upper-back rotation a full turn needs.

Endurance

Swinging as well on the 18th as on the 1st.

How it works

Screen it. Then train it.

  1. Rx01

    TPI 12-step screen

    Your coach runs TPI's 12-step physical assessment to find any weakness or limitation affecting your swing.

  2. Rx02

    Your 8-week plan

    A custom strength and conditioning program, built from exactly what the screen found.

  3. Rx03

    Private sessions

    Train with your coach once or twice a week for the first four weeks to lock in the movements.

  4. Rx04

    Maintenance

    Ongoing sessions after the first month to keep the improvement going through the season.
